If you're a creator or small business owner in India who's looked at Instagram DM automation, you've almost certainly hit **ManyChat**. It's the biggest name in the space, well-designed, and it works.

But here's what nobody tells you until after you sign up: ManyChat is optimized for US creators. If you're building your business from Mumbai, Bangalore, or a Tier-2 city, some things will feel off — pricing in USD, no UPI billing, no GST-compliant invoices, and support hours that don't match yours.

This post is an honest comparison. We'll cover where ManyChat is genuinely better, where alternatives (including our own product, DM Shiyam) win for Indian creators, and how to pick.

## What Indian creators actually need

Before comparing features, let's ground the criteria. From talking to hundreds of creators and small D2C brands in India, the recurring must-haves are:

1. **Pricing in INR** — not USD converted at checkout with a bank forex fee tacked on.
2. **UPI + Razorpay billing** — cards work, but UPI is what most Indians actually use.
3. **GST-compliant invoices** — needed to expense the tool for any registered business.
4. **Meta-approved automation** — the safe path, not scrapers.
5. **Support in your timezone** — because when a DM flow breaks at 11am IST, you need help now.
6. **A pricing tier that starts small** — most Indian creators aren't ready for a $25/month tool on day one.

Everything else is nice-to-have. Now let's compare.

- Billed in USD via card only. No UPI.
- Invoices are USD-denominated with no Indian GST breakdown.

## Compliance and account safety

Both tools use the official **Instagram Graph API** and are approved by Meta. Neither uses scrapers or unofficial endpoints. Neither will get your account banned when used correctly.

That said, watch out for the many "ManyChat alternatives" advertised on YouTube and Instagram itself that promise "unlimited free DMs" or "bulk cold outreach." Those tools almost always use scraping or unofficial APIs and *will* get you banned. If the tool doesn't have the Meta Tech Provider badge and hasn't passed App Review, avoid it.

## When you should still pick ManyChat

Be honest with yourself. Pick ManyChat if:

- You're running **WhatsApp + Messenger + Instagram** and need a single unified inbox.
- You've already built dozens of complex ManyChat flows and migration cost is real.
- You're on a US-registered entity and USD billing works fine for you.
- You need deep integrations with Klaviyo, HubSpot, or ActiveCampaign today.

For those cases, ManyChat is a mature choice.

## When a ManyChat alternative wins

Pick a leaner, India-first alternative if:

- **Cost matters** and you're paying out of pocket, not from a media budget.
- You want **UPI/Razorpay** and **GST invoices** without workarounds.
- You want a tool that **just does Instagram DMs really well**, without teaching you a flow-builder DSL.
- You value **local support** in your timezone and language.
- You're getting started and want to be running your first automation in 10 minutes, not 10 evenings.

## Migration is easier than you think

If you're already on ManyChat and considering switching:

- Export your flows as reference (screenshots are fine — the logic doesn't map 1:1 anyway).
- List your top 3–5 automations. Those are 80% of your DM volume.
- Rebuild them in the new tool. It's usually faster than migrating verbatim because the alternatives are more opinionated.
- Run both tools in parallel for 3 days to compare deliverability and reply rates.
- Cancel the old subscription when metrics match or beat.
